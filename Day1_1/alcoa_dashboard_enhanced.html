Help & Guide

About ALCOA+ Principles

ALCOA+ ensures that electronic records and signatures are reliable, authentic, and trustworthy. These principles are critical for regulatory compliance in pharmaceutical manufacturing.

The Six Principles:

  • Attributable: All data entries traced to individuals with timestamps
  • Legible: Data is clear, readable, and easily interpretable
  • Contemporaneous: Records made at the time events occur
  • Original: Original records preserved and protected
  • Accurate: Data verified for correctness and completeness
  • ALCOA+: Complete and consistent data formatting

How to Use This Dashboard

  1. Assessment Tab: Add equipment and select ALCOA+ principles that apply
  2. Equipment Registry: View all equipment, search, filter, and compare
  3. Analytics Tab: View compliance trends and detailed metrics
  4. Audit Trail: Track all modifications and assessments for compliance reporting
